The authors of this paper proposed that as gene lengths increased, finding the right shape of a large protein structure capable of regulating the gene became harder. Their model of gene growth is that of a multiplicative process. Thus, as gene length increased beyond a certain value eukaryotic organisms became favorable. They see that past the eukaryotic transition, the size of a protein expressed by a gene became almost fixed even though the gene size itself continued to grow. 

This marked an evolutionary #phase_transitions about 2 billion years ago. The increased gene length mostly meant there were more regulatory sequences that were added to the gene.
