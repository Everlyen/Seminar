Hierarchy: 
Top level pathways: Broad biological topics or superpathways

Sub-pathways: the individual pathways that are related to the top-level pathway 

Reactions: Individual molecular events and specific reactions

Recommendation: Enter through Immune system (click on the big immune system circle) and find the reactions which involve GBPs. You can click into reactions a lot more than you'd think.
