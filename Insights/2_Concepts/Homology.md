## What is it? 🤔
It is a property of RNA, DNA and protein sequences where two or more sequences share the same ancestor. The more similar the sequence, the more closely related 

They tend to have same structure because structure is more conserved than sequences. But not all similar structures need to be homologous, as in the case of convergent evolution.
