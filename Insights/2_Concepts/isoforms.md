## What is it? 🤔
Alternative forms of protein sequence that arise from alternative splicing of pre-mRNA. Alternative splicing is one form of RNA processing that happens to messanger RNA as it prepares to be translocated from the nucleus and transcribed in the cytosol. 

[[Alternative splicing]] removes (excises) non coding segments (introns) and the coding regions (exons) are joined together. Further capping of the 5' end of the mRNA and addition of a polyA tail to the 3' end results in mature mRNA. 

Constituitive vs. Alternative splicing: 
Constituitive: reads the code in sequenctial order. 
Alternative: selectively mixes, skips or keeps sections. 

### Increasing complexity
This means: 1 gene in the DNA can give rise to n forms of mRNA (isoforms). Each mRNA gives rise to a protein, which in turn can be post-translationally modified. Some isoforms are functionally different from each other. Others are associated with disease.
