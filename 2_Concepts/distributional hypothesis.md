## What is it ? 🤔
A principle of language modelling which states that a word (or a token) has its meaning derived from its context. The following quote captures the idea: 

> You shall know a word by the company it keeps
## Where I learnt this 🕶
Reading the paper [[Biological structure and function from learning sequences]]
## Example application 💡
Large Language Models learn representation of words and sequences through learning the context in which a word is usually present
