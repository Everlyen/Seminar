## What it is
A process that happens during gene expression where exons are included or excluded from the pre-mRNA transcript. Introns can be mistakenly or intentionally kept in the mRNA, exons are omitted, different boundaries are chosen for joining exons. 

The protein translation dogma: 
DNA (gene) -> pre-mRNA (this is where alternative splicing happens -> mRNA (messanger RNA) -> 

It allows the expression of different proteins from the same DNA sequence. This is how we have [~20 000 human protein coding genes and have ~90 000 distinct functional proteins](https://www.the-scientist.com/all-about-alternative-splicing-72189).
